# Skyfold — vision and pillars

## One line

A paper crane folded from a letter carries the last ember of a village lantern
across a broken paper sky.

## Player fantasy

You are small, light, and fragile in a world made of paper. The storm has
scattered the light, and the only way home is through it. Every flap is a choice
to spend height; every glide is a choice to trade control for speed. The world
opens in layers of torn paper, ink-wash weather, and gold-leaf lanterns.

## Audience

- Players who like one-more-run arcade games but want a authored journey.
- Players who play in short sessions on a laptop and want instant restart.
- Players who value a calm, tactile visual identity more than spectacle.
- Future mobile players, after the Windows release proves the feel.

## Pillars

1. **Flight feel first.** The flap–glide must feel better than any tap-only
   flyer. If the grey-box is not fun, stop and re-tune before producing content.
2. **Readable fairness.** Every failure is understood and earned. Telegraph
   hazards, keep hitboxes honest, and never kill from off-screen.
3. **A tactile paper world.** Gates are architecture, weather is a character,
   and the crane is a folded object with weight. The look is not a skin.
4. **A journey worth finishing.** Four biomes, 28 campaign nodes, a wordless
   story, and medals that make mastery visible.
5. **Respect the player.** Instant retry, accessibility, no ads, no telemetry,
   no accounts, and local saves.

## Core loop

- Tap to flap; hold after the flap to tuck into a glide; release to fall.
- Read the next gate, choose height, and thread the inner gap for score.
- Ride a wind ribbon or graze a gate edge for expression.
- Crash, understand why, and restart in under a second.
- Finish a passage, earn a medal, and unlock the next.

## The promise

A first-time player understands the controls in one run. A skilled player can
spend hours chasing cleaner threads, higher medals, and better daily runs. The
game looks and sounds finished, runs on ordinary Windows hardware, and never
wastes the player's time.

## Success criteria

- 80% of playtesters finish the first biome; 60% finish the campaign.
- Median attempts per passage at or below 5; no passage above 10.
- 80% of playtesters say they would play again.
- 60 fps p99 at 1080p on Intel Iris Xe.
- No P0/P1 defects at release.
- A clean Windows VM runs the public zip without Godot installed.
- A new player can install and understand the game from the README alone.

## Non-goals

Multiplayer, accounts, cloud saves, online leaderboards, ads, IAP, telemetry,
mobile/iOS/Android at v1.0, Steam/itch/Microsoft Store, level editor,
localization beyond English (strings still externalized), 3D, C#, addons, boss
combat, and any use of *Flappy Bird* intellectual property.

## Title

`Skyfold` is the working public title. It must clear a trademark and search
check before public release. `Featherline` is the fallback. The repository name
is `game-skyfold`; `game-flappy-bird` remains only an internal planning label.
